Q&A Study Notes: Visual Metaphor

What are the important skills needed when designing information graphics?
Designers need the ability to express ideas through symbols, icons, illustrations, and typography. They must also establish hierarchies of visual importance, create effective visual metaphors, and translate information into functional, beautiful visual forms.
Why is visual communication so important?
It is difficult for viewers to understand and digest large amounts of data. Communication design relies on smart solutions to simplify and deliver messages effectively.
What is a Visual Metaphor?
It is the representation of a person, place, thing, or idea using a visual image that suggests a particular association or point of similarity. It is a critical tool to ensure that the intended meaning of a message is clearly understood by the recipient.
How do shapes communicate visually?
Simple geometric shapes carry historical and psychological meanings:
  • Circles: Communicate wholeness or unity.
  • Triangles: Communicate energetic and direct movement.
  • Squares: Communicate stability and strength.
  • Lines: Variations in thickness and stroke can create depth and direct movement.
Why do some information graphics fail?
A major challenge is maintaining a sense of beauty. Information graphics often fail because they are visually too busy, and the human eye can only process a limited amount of data at one time.
How does color play a role in visual metaphors?
Understanding how humans perceive and classify colors allows designers to make strategic decisions on how to refine motion graphic elements to evoke the right emotions (e.g., blue for trust, red for energy, green for eco-friendly).

Answers to the End-of-Lesson Questions

1. What are the important skills to have when designing information graphic?
The important skills include the ability to:
  • Express ideas through symbols, icons, illustrations, and typography.
  • Establish hierarchies of visual importance.
  • Create effective visual metaphors.
  • Translate information into beautiful visual forms.
2. Why visual information is important?
Visual information is important because it can be difficult for viewers to understand and digest large amounts of data. Translating this data into functional and beautiful visual forms simplifies the data and helps deliver messages effectively.
3. What is the visual metaphor?
A visual metaphor is the representation of a person, place, thing, or idea by means of a visual image that suggests a particular association or point of similarity.
4. List down the type of visual that can be used to represent metaphor.
Types of visuals that can be used include:
  • Iconography (icons and symbols).
  • Pictorial metaphors (everyday, tactile objects).
  • Simple geometric shapes (circles, triangles, squares, lines).
  • Colors.
  • Stock visual metaphors (such as standard emojis or memes).
5. How to develop a visual metaphor?
To develop a visual metaphor, you can repurpose everyday objects and compose them to signify your data. You must design a logical system that communicates the information effectively. Using unexpected visual metaphors can pleasantly surprise the viewer and capture their attention. Additionally, you should make strategic decisions regarding the use of shapes and colors based on their psychological meanings to refine the overall message.
