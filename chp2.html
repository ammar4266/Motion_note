Q&A Study Notes: Visual Storytelling

What are the roles and elements of motion graphics in social media?
Their roles are explaining concepts, branding, marketing, and creating awareness. Key elements include explainer videos, GIFs, UI animations, icons, infographics, and logos.
What is the purpose and framework of Visual Storytelling?
The purpose is to get attention, tell a story, trigger emotions, and drive quick action. The framework relies on four core elements: Message, Conflict, Characters, and Plot.
How are core messages categorized?
  • To pitch: Focuses on goals and what you do.
  • To report: Focuses on what happened and its impact.
  • To sell: Focuses on products, problems, and results.
  • To present: Focuses on a topic or issue.
What are common character goals and emotional needs?
Goals include winning, escaping death, stopping something bad, retrieving, or delivering. Emotional needs include certainty, variety, significance, connection, growth, and contribution.
What are the three game narrative types?
  • Linear: Fixed events in the same order.
  • Branching: Player choices create multiple story paths.
  • Open: Players freely explore and trigger story blocks in any order.
How can you generate new story ideas?
Use personal experience, news, or combine different concepts. Ask "What if...?" and change one element of a familiar story. Always avoid your very first idea, as it is usually a cliché.

Answers to the End-of-Lesson Questions

1. What are the roles of motion graphics in social media?
The roles of motion graphics in social media include explaining concepts, branding, marketing, and creating awareness.
2. What is the main purpose of Visual Storytelling?
The main purpose of visual storytelling is to get people's attention, tell your story, trigger their emotions, and activate them to take action quickly.
3. What is the type of Core messages in motion graphics?
Core messages are categorized into four types: "To pitch", "To report", "To sell", and "To present".
4. List down the character's goal in visual narration.
A character's goals typically fall into these categories:
  • To Win.
  • To Escape Death.
  • To Stop (something bad from happening).
  • To Retrieve (a treasure or person).
  • To Deliver (a person or thing).
5. List down three types of narration in computer games.
The three types of narration in computer games are:
  • Linear Narratives.
  • Branching Narratives.
  • Open Narratives.
